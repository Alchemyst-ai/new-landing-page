# What is Pullback Trading?

A pullback trading strategy involves buying during temporary market declines within an uptrend. This approach allows traders to enter positions at more favorable prices while maintaining the overall bullish perspective.

## Key Components of Pullback Trading

1. **Trend Identification**: First, identify the primary trend (uptrend in this case)
2. **Support Levels**: Look for key support levels where price might bounce
3. **Entry Timing**: Wait for confirmation of the pullback ending
4. **Risk Management**: Set clear stop-loss levels below support

## When to Use Pullback Trading

- During strong uptrends
- When price pulls back to major support levels
- After confirmation of trend continuation
- When volume supports the move

## Example Trade Setup

Let's look at a practical example of a pullback trade:

1. Market shows clear uptrend
2. Price pulls back to support
3. Wait for candlestick confirmation
4. Enter when price shows signs of reversal
5. Set stop-loss below recent low

Remember, successful pullback trading requires patience and discipline. Don't chase the market - wait for the right setup to present itself.
